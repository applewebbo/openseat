# News and Information

Besides the form and the events, the public site offers two reference
pages.

## Info Page

An optional page with the association's contact details — address, tax
code, email — and the link to the statute, plus a free-form text the
association can write in admin (its own story, its team, anything that does
not fit the fixed fields). It only shows if the association chooses to
publish it.

![The info page](../assets/screenshots/info-page.png)

## Releases Page

A plain-language changelog, written for non-technical readers: every
released version appears with its most relevant changes, written out in
prose. The link sits in the footer of every page, next to the version
number.

![The releases page](../assets/screenshots/releases-page.png)
