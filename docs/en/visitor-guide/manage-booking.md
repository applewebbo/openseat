# Managing Your Booking

Right after booking, the confirmation page lists everyone booked for that
event, with two actions for each: **Edit** and **Cancel**.

- **Edit** goes back to choosing who is attending, to add or remove someone
  from the household
- **Cancel** frees the place; the booking stays on record as cancelled, it
  is not deleted — so the association knows it existed and when it was
  withdrawn

## If You Lost the Link

Every booking confirmation arrives by email with a personal link that always
leads back to managing your bookings for that event. If the link cannot be
found, the **Manage your bookings** page lets you request a new one by
email.

![The page for recovering the link to your bookings](../assets/screenshots/event-recover.png)

Just enter the email address used when booking: if active bookings exist for
that address, an email arrives with the links to each of them.
