# Applying for Membership

Anyone who wants to join fills in the public application form — a link the
association publishes on its own site, on social media, or shares by email.
No account is needed.

## Where to Find It

The application form lives at its own address (for example
`/modulo/adesione/`), with a title and introduction the association writes
in admin. Before starting, the page lists what to have at hand: the tax code
and address of whoever is applying, and — if it is for a minor — the same
details for the child.

![The opening page of the application form](../assets/screenshots/join-landing.png)

## The Path

The form moves one section at a time, never all on a single page:

1. Who the application is for (yourself, a minor child, someone you are the
   legal guardian of)
2. Your own anagraphic details
3. The details of whoever is joining, if different from your own
4. Accepting the statute and the membership fee
5. The privacy notice
6. Consents to publish photos and videos
7. Final review and signature

Each association may have customised the titles, the text, and even added
its own questions to these sections — what is actually shown depends on how
the form was configured.

!!! tip "Drafts save themselves"
    There is no need to finish everything in one sitting: answers are saved
    at every step. Closing the page is fine — an email link lets you pick up
    where you left off within thirty days; past that, the draft is no longer
    valid and has to start over.

## After Sending

Once the last page is signed, the application is sent and a confirmation
email follows. If a second parent's consent is also needed, that person
receives a separate email with their own consent to give.
